# KRY | LIVI

> KRY International AB is Europe's largest digital-first healthcare provider, founded in Stockholm in 2015 by Johannes Schildt. It delivers primary and specialist care through a mobile and web app — video and chat consultations with doctors, nurses, psychologists and physiotherapists — combined with a network of physical clinics. The company trades as KRY in Sweden, Norway and Germany and as Livi in the United Kingdom and France, reporting 14M+ patient appointments, 4,000+ healthcare professionals and 60+ clinics.

KRY | LIVI publishes **no public API, developer portal, API reference, SDKs or OpenAPI specification**. Its consumer surface is the KRY / Livi apps; its business surface (NHS, Swedish regions, insurers, employers, Employee Assistance Programmes) is sold and integrated under contract. Patient authentication in Sweden uses BankID. Its published vulnerability disclosure policy names APIs as in scope, but those APIs are not documented publicly.
